# Monetra

Monetra is a simple, mobile-friendly app for keeping track of your money, purchases, vehicle loan, and activities—all in one place.

- **Home:** Keep track of account balances and hide them when you want privacy.
- **Value:** See how much your purchases cost per day of ownership.
- **Vehicle:** Follow your loan balance and estimated payment progress.
- **Activities:** Keep upcoming plans and past activities together.
- **Profiles:** Create separate profiles with their own data.

Open Monetra in your browser to get started. Add it to your Home Screen for an app-like experience. Once the initial online setup finishes, you can use it offline too.

No sign-up needed. Your data stays in your browser on your device and does not sync elsewhere. Clearing browser data will erase your saved information.
